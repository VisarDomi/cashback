# Loyalty Aggregator Research

## What Is a Loyalty Aggregator?

A loyalty aggregator is an intermediary platform that provides consumers with a consolidated, single view of multiple loyalty programs. Key distinction from related models:

| Model | How It Works | Key Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Loyalty Aggregator | Third-party app that consolidates existing loyalty cards/programs into one view | Passive consolidation, no formal partnership needed |
| Coalition Loyalty Program | Multiple brands collaborate under shared framework with common currency (e.g., Payback points) | Active collaboration, brands jointly issue shared currency |
| Cashback App | Cash rebates on purchases at partner merchants, funded by affiliate revenue | Monetary return, not points-based |
| Card-Linked Offers | Rewards triggered automatically when linked card used at partner | No scanning needed, tied to payment instrument |

## Why Businesses Join Coalitions

### Cost Comparison
- Build your own loyalty program Year 1: ~$550,000
- Join a coalition Year 1: ~$29,000
- 73.2% of small business owners operate WITHOUT a formal loyalty program (SumUp survey)

### Benefits
- Shared customer acquisition costs across network
- Cross-sector data insights
- Enterprise-grade technology at fraction of cost
- Coalition solves "cold start" — new members from any partner available to all

## Why Consumers Need Aggregators (Loyalty Fatigue)

- Average consumer: 19 loyalty memberships, only 9.3 active
- 81% of consumers are members of at least one program
- 57% will abandon a program if it takes too long to earn
- 85% of members haven't heard from the program since signup
- ~$48 billion in reward points issued annually, at least 1/3 unredeemed
- 65% of consumers report being reliant on loyalty programs amid economic pressures

## Emerging Markets Gap

- Balkans: virtually no coalition loyalty platforms
- Eastern Europe: Payback operates in Poland, Croatia, Slovenia only
- Middle East loyalty market growing at 13.8% CAGR toward $3.27B in 2025
- Africa: predominantly mobile-money-driven, not traditional coalitions
- Southeast Asia: super-apps (Grab, Gojek, Shopee) function as de facto loyalty ecosystems

## Key Takeaways

1. Global loyalty market: $214.7B by 2028 at 9.2% CAGR
2. Traditional coalitions declining in West, thriving in Asia and Middle East
3. Biggest gap: small/emerging markets like Balkans — no coalition platforms
4. 73% of small businesses worldwide have no loyalty program
5. Super-apps are the new coalitions in emerging markets
6. Loyalty fatigue (19 memberships, 9 active) creates demand for consolidation
