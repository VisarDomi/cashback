Grant Pitfalls & Co-founder Alignment

What to resolve before the application · Updated March 15, 2026

1. Budget Caps Are Negotiable, Not Fixed

KEY INSIGHT The 25%/15%/10% figures in the regulation are reference starting points, not hard limits.

From the regulation (Section 6): percentages can change based on project goals. Every line item must be justified (value, timeline, budget). The evaluation committee can negotiate amounts within their ceilings to better reflect the project.

CategoryReference %Reference Max (42K)Reality
Staff/salaries (gross incl. taxes)25%~10,500Negotiable upward if justified
Expert services / R&D / testing10%~4,200Negotiable
Sales & marketing15%~6,300Negotiable
Goods & services for developmentNo cap listed—Hardware, SaaS, app store fees
General expenses (subscriptions, tools)No cap listed—Cloud, domains, monitoring
IP protection (patents, trademarks)No cap listed—Trademark registration

Pitfall: Assuming staff is hard-capped at 25% and building the budget around that. The sole developer IS the primary cost — argue for a higher staff allocation in the application.

3. Vesting: Equity Is Earned, Not Gifted

Without vesting: someone quits after 2 weeks and keeps 33% forever.

With vesting: equity unlocks over time. Leave early = keep only what you earned.

Proposed structure for 8-month grant period:

PeriodWhat happens
Month 1-3Cliff Nobody owns anything yet. Leave = 0% equity.
Month 4Cliff vests 50% of promised equity unlocks (16.5% of 33%).
Month 5-8Monthly vesting ~4.125% additional per month.
Month 8Fully vested Everyone who stayed has their full share.

4. Deliverables: Promise Conservative, Deliver Adequate

CRITICAL You define your own deliverables in the application. The committee approves them. Then you're held to what you promised. Over-promising to score higher = under-delivering at tranche reporting.

The grant evaluates a Phase 2 (validation) startup. They expect market validation, not a finished product.

What the state expects

  • Did you build what you said you'd build?
  • Does it show market potential?
  • Were the funds spent as budgeted?
  • Is there a path to commercialization?

What the state does NOT expect

  • Profitability
  • Thousands of users
  • Completed banking integration
  • A polished fintech product

Conservative deliverables per tranche

Tranche 1 — 30% (~12.6K EUR)

After contract signing

  • Company registered (LLC)
  • Production DB, auth, CI/CD
  • Development environment live

Translation: "we started"

Tranche 2 — 40% (~16.8K EUR)

After phase 1 + financial report

  • Working app with real users (even 50)
  • At least 1 merchant onboarded
  • Receipt scanning on fiskalizimi
  • App Store / Play Store presence (beta OK)
  • Banking partner conversations documented

Tranche 3 — 30% (~12.6K EUR)

After final financial report

  • App live with real transactions
  • Multiple merchants
  • Revenue model validated (small revenue OK)
  • Full financial documentation for KLSH

5. Grant Contract & Liability

The regulation (Section 22-24) specifies:

  • Grant contract signed within 30 working days of winner list approval
  • Contract defines: rules, deadlines, responsibilities, reporting timelines, financial descriptions, fund transfer methods, eligible expenses
  • Financial records must be available to Kontrolli i Lartë i Shtetit (Supreme Audit Institution)
  • Monitoring includes document verification and field visits

UNRESOLVED Questions for the meeting:

  • Who signs the grant contract? The company? An individual co-founder?
  • Who is personally liable if deliverables aren't met?
  • What are the clawback mechanics? (Not explicit in regulation — defined in the contract)
  • If one co-founder exits mid-grant, does it affect the contract?

6. Exit Scenarios

DEV EXITS

  • Vested equity stays with dev
  • Code is company IP — no dispute
  • Remaining founders continue with grant
  • Define: transition period length
  • Define: buyback formula for vested shares

CO-FOUNDER EXITS

  • Vested equity stays, unvested forfeited
  • Remaining founders can buy out at agreed formula
  • Buyback formula TBD: invested cash + (below-market salary × months worked)

PROJECT DIES

  • Grant funds already disbursed — must account for them per contract
  • KLSH audit exposure
  • Company dissolved per Albanian LLC rules
  • Remaining assets (if any) split by equity

GRANT NOT AWARDED

  • Project continues as side project (Scenario C)
  • No financial obligations
  • All co-founders coast at agreed pace
  • Re-evaluate at next grant cycle

9. The 15% Co-financing Question

Grant covers 85%. The remaining 15% (~6,300 EUR) must come from the founders.

Eligible sources: personal funds, operating revenue, private investment, equity financing, or any legal source excluding other state funding in the same fiscal year.

RESOLVE IN MEETING Who pays the 6,300 EUR? Split equally (2,100 each)? Proportional to equity? One co-founder covers it? This needs an answer before the application.
