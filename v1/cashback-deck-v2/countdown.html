What's Defensible vs. What's Arbitrary

Defensible — grounded in data or existing work

  • Risk matrix — risks come directly from the cost analysis. Single developer, banking dependency, regulatory unknowns, market adoption. Already analyzed, just needs structuring.
  • Team section — dev CV is ready, just need co-founder narratives. Factual, easy to write.
  • Competitive analysis — verifiable claim. Either someone else does fiskalizimi cashback or nobody does. A few hours of research confirms it.
  • Budget — the cost analysis exists. Restructuring into grant categories is formatting work, not guesswork. Use $20/hr Albanian rate, Albanian hardware prices.

Needs real work — can't be faked or estimated

  • Revenue model — requires actual merchant fee research, receipt volume data, realistic conversion rates. Finance guy must own this. Guessing numbers will hurt at evaluation.
  • Market sizing — needs INSTAT data, tax authority numbers, smartphone stats. Can't make up "100K businesses use fiskalizimi" without a source. Committee will check.
  • Co-founder CVs — only they can write them. No substitute.
